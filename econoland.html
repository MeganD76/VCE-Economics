Economics glossary
Consumption (C)
Household spending on goods and services. The largest component of AD.
Leakage (S)
Money that flows out of the spending cycle. Savings, taxes and imports are leakages.
Disposable income
Income remaining after tax is deducted. What households can actually spend or save.
Opportunity cost
The value of the next best alternative forgone when making a decision.

Economics glossary
Investment (I)
Business spending on capital goods — machinery, equipment, buildings. An injection into the circular flow.
Hurdle rate
The minimum rate of return a business requires before committing to an investment project.
Revenue
Total income received from selling goods and services. Revenue minus costs equals profit.
Operating loss
When costs exceed revenue. A business running losses must reduce spending or risk closure.

Economics glossary
Government spending (G)
An injection into the circular flow. Government purchases goods/services from businesses or makes transfer payments to households.
Taxation (T)
A leakage from the circular flow. Reduces household disposable income and business revenue available for spending.
Budget surplus
When T > G. Government collects more than it spends — net leakage from the economy.
Budget deficit
When G > T. Government spends more than it collects — net injection into the economy.
