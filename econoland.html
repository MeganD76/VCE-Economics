Economics glossary
Opportunity cost
The value of the next best alternative forgone when making a decision.
Discretionary income
Disposable income minus committed expenses. What you can freely choose to spend.
Leakage (S, T, M)
Money that flows out of the domestic spending cycle. Reduces AD.
Injection (I, G, X)
Money that enters the circular flow from outside household spending. Increases AD.
